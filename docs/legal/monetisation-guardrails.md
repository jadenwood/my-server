# Donations and monetisation guardrails

What Realm may and may not do with money. Read with the [`eula-compliance-checklist.md`](eula-compliance-checklist.md) (rows E6-E9) and the [`rights-holder-outreach-letter.md`](rights-holder-outreach-letter.md).

> **Not legal advice.** Whether the Reign of Kings EULA is "noncommercial", and what it counts as commercial, is **UNVERIFIED**: nobody on the project has read its text (checklist §0). Until it has been read **and** the answers recorded, the strictest column below applies to everything. Tax, consumer-protection and charity rules in the Owner's country also apply and are not covered here.

---

## 1. Current rule (in force now)

From `docs/community/launch-plan.md` §0, unchanged: **no money of any kind, and no paid perks of any kind.** No donations, no supporter tiers, no sponsored events. That stays until both of these are done:

1. The EULA's rows E6-E9 are filled in from the real text.
2. Where this page says "needs written permission", that permission is in hand and recorded (checklist §5).

## 2. The three tiers

Assuming the EULA turns out to allow personal, non-commercial use and says nothing more specific (the common case for game EULAs, but **UNVERIFIED** here):

| Tier | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Never** | Anything that sells the game, its content or an advantage in it | Not allowed, even with permission, because it breaks Realm's own rules |
| **B. Needs written permission** from the rights holder | Anything that brings in money because of the game, even just to cover costs | Allowed only once permission is in writing |
| **C. Allowed without permission** | Things that are not money for Realm | Allowed now |

### A. Never

- Selling, or giving for donations, **any in-game item, resource, house, title, crown time, renown, dynasty prestige, treasury marks, ransom release, event prize, protection, queue priority or whitelist place**. This is pay-to-win and real-money trading (`docs/community/rules.md` §3).
- **Paid access** to a server, or a paid whitelist.
- **Selling or renting game copies, keys, accounts** or server slots.
- Loot boxes, raffles or anything chance-based for money.
- Selling **merchandise using the game's name, logo or art**.
- Accepting money from anyone **in return for a moderation decision**, an unban or a favourable ruling.
- Advertising inside the game (server notices, `/notice`, plugin messages, house names) for money.

### B. Needs written permission first

| Activity | Extra conditions even with permission |
|---|---|
| **Donations to cover running costs** (hosting, domain, backups, code-signing certificate) | Donations buy **nothing**: no role, no perk, no priority. Publish a simple monthly ledger: costs, donations, balance. Money above costs is held for future costs or refunded, never paid to people. Use a platform that lets donors see it is voluntary. Say "Donations cover server costs only and give no in-game benefit" next to every donate link |
| **Cosmetic recognition** for donors **outside** the game (a "patrons of the realm" list on the portal, a Discord role colour with no permissions) | Nothing in game. Opt-in, so donors can stay anonymous. No tiers that rank people by amount |
| **Sponsored events** (a sponsor pays for a Crown Night) | The sponsor gets a mention only, outside the game. No sponsor influence on rules, results or moderation. Disclose it in the announcement |
| **Using the game's name** in Realm's branding beyond a plain description | Realm already avoids this. "A community server for Reign of Kings" is the only wording to ask for |
| **Realm's own merchandise** using only Realm's original art and lore, no game name or art | Ask anyway: it is sold because of the community around the game |
| **Paying a host on behalf of others** (Realm rents a server and asks members to chip in) | Treat as donations above |

### C. Allowed now, without permission

- Spending the Owner's **own money** on hosting, and saying so.
- Free giveaways of things the giver owns **outside the game** (a creator's own merch, a game key the giver bought), with no purchase needed to enter, following the giveaway platform's and local rules.
- **Creators' own channels:** whether a creator may earn from Reign of Kings footage is between the creator, the platform and the rights holder (EULA row E8). Realm neither allows nor forbids it, and Realm takes no share. Creators must follow [`../community/ops/creator-code-of-conduct.md`](../community/ops/creator-code-of-conduct.md) §4.
- Code signing, hosting and domain bills paid by the Owner. Those are costs, not income.

## 3. Rules that apply to any money

1. **Open books.** If money is ever handled, a public monthly ledger (in summary; never donors' names without consent).
2. **Separate money.** Use an account or platform wallet used only for Realm. No staff member keeps donations personally.
3. **No obligation.** Never pressure, tease or reward donations in chat, in game or on stream.
4. **Refunds.** A donor who asks within 14 days gets a refund. Say so next to the donate link.
5. **No secret deals.** Any sponsorship or payment to staff or creators connected to Realm is told to the other staff.
6. **Stop on request.** If the rights holder asks Realm to stop any money activity, it stops that day, and remaining funds are used for costs already owed or refunded.
7. **Tax and law.** The person receiving money is responsible for any tax or legal duties where they live. Check before the first donation. **Not covered here.**

| Can we add a "Donate" button to the portal? | No | Yes, with the donations conditions in B |
