# Rights-holder outreach letter (template)

A polite letter asking the holder of the Reign of Kings rights for **written permission** for the Realm community revival. Send it before going public (launch plan Phase 3) and before anything involving money. Keep it short; the rights holder owes us nothing.

> **Not legal advice.** A permission email is useful evidence of good faith, but it is not a contract unless it says so. If the reply grants commercial rights, or asks you to sign something, have a lawyer read it first.

---

## Before you send

1. **Find out who holds the rights today.** The store page names the developer as Code Hatch (`README.md`). Whether Code Hatch is still trading, was sold, or has passed the rights to a publisher or someone else is **UNVERIFIED**. Check, in this order:
   - the Steam store page's Developer and Publisher fields, and the "End User License Agreement" link (it names the licensor);
   - the copyright line in the game's own credits or launcher;
   - the company's own website and social accounts, and the public company register in its country.
   Write down what you found and where. Address the letter to that entity, not to a person you guessed.
2. **Use a public contact route** they publish (a business or support email, a contact form, or the Steam developer page). Do not message individual current or former employees on their personal accounts.
3. **Read the EULA first** ([`eula-compliance-checklist.md`](eula-compliance-checklist.md) §0) so the letter asks only for what is actually needed.
4. **Tick only the requests you need** (the bracketed list in the letter). Fewer asks get faster answers.
5. Fill in every `[bracket]`. Remove any request you do not need, and the brackets.
6. Send it from an address you will keep. Keep a copy of what you sent and the date.

## The letter

> **Subject:** Permission request: non-commercial community server for Reign of Kings ("Realm")
>
> Dear [Rights holder name] team,
>
> My name is [Your name]. I run a small, volunteer community project called **Realm**: a set of community servers for **Reign of Kings**, with an original story setting (the realm of Ostreval and its own houses), and open-source tools that help players find and join our servers.
>
> We are writing because we want to do this properly and with your blessing.
>
> **What Realm is**
> - Every player uses **their own copy of Reign of Kings bought on Steam**. We do not distribute, host, repackage or patch the game client, and we never will.
> - Our servers run the Reign of Kings dedicated server, installed through Steam. Server-side features come from the open-source Oxide modding framework and our own server plugins. **No changes are made to players' game files or to the anti-cheat.**
> - Our lore, names, art and logo are all original. We say clearly everywhere that Realm is an **unofficial community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by [Rights holder name]**.
> - Realm is free to play on. Today it makes no money of any kind.
> - About our community: [number] players in testing, [Discord member count], [link to portal or Discord].
>
> **What we are asking for**
>
> We would be grateful for your written permission for the following. [Keep only the items you need.]
>
> 1. **Community servers:** to run [up to N] public Reign of Kings dedicated servers for the Realm community, using the official dedicated server with server-side modifications (Oxide and our plugins), as described above.
> 2. **Name and description:** to describe Realm as "a community server for Reign of Kings" and to use the game's name (not its logo) for that purpose on our website, Discord and player app.
> 3. **Donations to cover costs:** to accept voluntary donations that cover only server hosting and related running costs, with **no in-game items or advantages** in return, and with costs and donations published openly.
> 4. **Cosmetic, non-advantage supporter recognition:** [for example a name in a "patrons of the realm" list on our website, never in-game advantages]. [Delete if not needed.]
> 5. **Videos and streams:** to let our community's creators stream and post videos of play on our servers, [including monetised videos on their own channels]. [Delete if not needed or if your EULA already covers it.]
>
> **What we will always do**
> - Keep the game client untouched and require every player to own the game on Steam.
> - Never sell in-game items, advantages or access.
> - Show a clear "unofficial, not affiliated" notice and credit Reign of Kings and [Rights holder name].
> - Stop or change anything you are not comfortable with, as soon as you ask.
>
> If you would like to see anything before deciding, our code and documents are here: [repo or portal link, if public]. I am happy to answer questions or to make changes.
>
> If you are not the right people to ask, could you point us to whoever is? And if the answer is no, we would still be grateful to hear it, so that we can respect it.
>
> Thank you for making Reign of Kings. Its players are still keeping it alive.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Your name]
> [Role, for example "Owner, Realm community servers"]
> [Contact email]
> [Country, which can matter for which law applies]

## After you send

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| No reply in 30 days | Send one short, polite follow-up quoting the first message. After that, do not chase. Silence is **not** permission: carry on only with what the EULA already allows, and keep "no money" in force |
| A "yes" | Save the full reply privately (with headers). Record who answered, their role, the date and exactly what was allowed in the EULA checklist §5. Do not stretch it: a "yes" to donations is not a "yes" to supporter perks |
| A "yes, with conditions" | Follow the conditions exactly; update [`monetisation-guardrails.md`](monetisation-guardrails.md) and the creator code |
| A "no" or a request to stop | Stop the named activity at once, tell the community plainly and without blame, and record it. If they ask the servers to stop, the Owner decides how quickly to wind down and how to export the Chronicle and Hall of Kings for players to keep |
| A request to sign a licence or pay a fee | Do not sign before a lawyer has read it |

Never claim to have permission you do not have in writing, and never publish the rights holder's reply without asking them.
