# Creator and streamer code of conduct

For anyone who streams, records or posts videos of the Realm server. It builds on the player rules ([`rules.md`](../rules.md), especially §5 on sniping and ghosting) and the practical [`streamer-kit.md`](../streamer-kit.md). Where they overlap, the stricter one applies.

> Not legal advice. Whether you may monetise videos of Reign of Kings is a question for the game's rights holder and your platform. See [`../../legal/monetisation-guardrails.md`](../../legal/monetisation-guardrails.md) §4.

Creators are welcome, and the Chronicle and overlay exist for them. In return, a creator's audience is bigger than one player's voice, so the rules for creators are a little stricter.

---

## 1. Honesty about what Realm is

- Say plainly, in your title, description or on screen: the game is **Reign of Kings, bought on Steam**; Realm is a **community server** that does not change game files; Realm is **not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's developers or publisher** (the same line the trailer end card uses, `trailer-script.md`).
- Do not call Realm "official", "the new Reign of Kings", a "remaster" or a "sequel".
- Do not use the game's logo or the developer's branding as if it were Realm's. Use Realm's own art (`art/`, `docs/brand.md`).
- If Realm staff gave you anything (early access to an event, a featured slot in an announcement), say so.

## 2. Fair play on stream

- **No stream sniping and no ghosting**, in either direction. Do not act on another stream, and do not let your chat feed you information (`rules.md` §5).
- **Moderate your chat** for coordinates, base locations and live callouts. Set your chat rules to say so. Recommended: a stream delay of at least 2 minutes (`streamer-kit.md`).
- **Never point your audience at a player.** No "go tell X", no raids into a rival's stream to cause trouble, no asking viewers to report someone you are fighting.
- **Exploits are never content.** If something breaks on air (a duplication, a stuck captive, a throne capture that should have been blocked), stop, do not repeat it, and tell staff privately. Do not show the method. Showing how to reproduce an exploit is treated as spreading it ([`incident-response.md`](incident-response.md)).
- **Staff creators** hold no plugin admin permission while streaming competitive play (`staff-roles-and-permissions.md` §4). Admin views (alerts, evidence, ravens audit, the Court) must **never** be on screen.

## 3. Respect for the people in your videos

- **Characters, yes; people, no.** Betrayal on stream is content. Mocking the real person behind a character (their voice, accent, age, skill, identity) is not.
- **Ask before featuring someone as a person** in a title, thumbnail or a clip about them. Featuring their character in the Chronicle or a recap is fine.
- **Voice chat:** tell people when they join your call that they are on stream. Do not broadcast another player's voice when they have asked you not to; mute or cut it in the edit.
- **Do not show private information:** another player's Discord DMs, raven letters sent to you privately, their Steam profile, real name, location or anything they did not choose to make public.
- **Minors:** if a player says or seems to be under 18, do not single them out, do not feature them as a person, and keep your chat away from them.
- **Take it down on request** when a player asks you to remove a clip that features them as a person (not just their character in a public event). Staff will ask you to as well when a harassment case needs it.

## 4. Money, sponsorship and giveaways

Until the Owner has written permission from the rights holder (see `monetisation-guardrails.md`):

- **Never sell or promise anything in game** for money, subs, follows, donations or channel points: no items, houses, titles, crown time, ransom releases, priority joins or favours. This is real-money trading (`rules.md` §3) and gets a permanent ban.
- **No sub-only or donor-only in-game perks**, and no "the viewer who donates most picks the next king".
- **Giveaways** may be of things you own outside the game (your own merch, a game key you bought). They may not involve in-game items, Realm staff powers or Realm itself as the prize. Follow your platform's and your country's giveaway rules.
- **Sponsored segments** must not say or imply that Realm, the game's rights holder or the sponsor endorses the other. Disclose sponsorships as your platform and local law require.
- Whether your own channel may earn ad revenue from Reign of Kings footage depends on the game's terms and your platform's rules, not on Realm. **UNVERIFIED** for this game: nobody on the project has read the rights holder's video policy (see `eula-compliance-checklist.md`).

## 5. Breaking this code

Breaches are handled with the normal ladder (`moderation-handbook.md` §2), plus:

| Breach | Usual result |
|---|---|
| Missing the "not affiliated" statement, or calling Realm official | A request to fix it; repeated refusal ends Realm featuring you |
| Showing admin views or another player's private information | Clip down within 24 h; level 2-4 depending on harm |
| Directing viewers at a player, or sniping or ghosting | Level 4 to start (`moderation-handbook.md`) |
| Selling in-game advantages, or showing how to reproduce an exploit | Level 6 (permanent) |

Realm may stop featuring a creator (announcements, the creator list) without any in-game penalty. That is not appealable, but in-game penalties are, through [`ban-appeals.md`](ban-appeals.md).

## 6. Sign-off

Creators taking part in a creator night (launch plan Phase 2) confirm in the creator channel: "I have read the Realm creator code of conduct and will follow it." Staff note the date.
