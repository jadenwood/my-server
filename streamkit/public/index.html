Realm Stream Scenes

OBS browser sources for the Chronicle of Ostreval. Add each as a Browser source at 1920 × 1080. Every scene reads the local chronicle through this server, so OBS needs nothing else. Full options: streamkit/README.md.

  • War BoardHouses, liege lines, treaties and claims as a live graph. /war-board
  • Throne RoomCoronation lower third with an animated sigil. /throne-room
  • Breaking NewsAlert queue for betrayals, claims and rebellions. /breaking-news
  • CountdownTime to the next Lawful Hours, claim or realm event. /countdown
  • Starting SoonFull-screen holding scene. /starting-soon

Common URL options

scale=1.25Enlarge or shrink the scene's content (0.25 to 4).
transparent=0Draw a backdrop (for previewing in a browser). Overlays are transparent by default.
house=Varrow,CorvaneOnly these houses (and, on the War Board, the houses they touch).
motion=0Reduced motion: fades only. The OS "reduce motion" setting does the same.
replay=NPlay the last N matching events on load, for positioning.
