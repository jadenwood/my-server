Logo

Use the dark versions on iron and dark photos, and the light versions on parchment or white. Keep clear space of half the height of the REALM capitals on every side. The one-colour emblem takes the text colour; in an image tag it falls back to black, as shown here.

Event icons

One icon for each of the Chronicle's event types (listed under each icon). 24px grid, 1.75 stroke, currentColor. They take the text colour of wherever they are placed. A sprite of all icons is at sprite/icons.svg; a contact sheet is at png/icons/contact-sheet.png.

Key art

The Old Throne on its hill above the Hearth at dusk, with the banners of the six great houses along the road. For the launcher, the portal and Discord: 1920 × 1080 for splash screens and headers, 1200 × 630 for link previews. The -title versions carry the logo on a dark scrim.

Season badges

One medal per season: the numeral in gold on an enamel that cycles every four seasons. 256px, with 64px PNGs.

Renown title badges

One badge for each default title in RealmRenown. Honours are gold on iron; infamous titles are bone on blood in blackened iron. Titles a server adds in the plugin config have no badge until one is added to src/titles.json.

Textures

Tileable 512px patterns for backgrounds: background: #ecdfbf url(textures/parchment.svg) and background: #131417 url(textures/iron.svg).

Discord

Upload the PNGs, not the SVGs. The server icon is shown as a circle. Sigil PNGs at 128px (png/sigils/*-128.png) work as server emoji.

Social card

1200 × 630, for link previews and announcements. The template's text needs Cinzel and EB Garamond; post the PNG. Make a filled card with:

node art/tools/build.mjs card --house dunmere --kicker "Claim declared" \
  --title "Dunmere raises a claim at the Hearth" --subtitle "The Lawful Hours open on Saturday." \
  --footer "The Chronicle of Ostreval" --out card.svg --png card.png
